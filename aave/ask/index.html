How the calculation works

221 planned days = 261 weekdays in 2027 − 5 public holidays − 35 leave days. Holidays and leave never overlap in this estimate.

Employee reference days = 261 minus any quantified, non-overlapping annual PTO and holiday days in the source. Missing counts use the mean of the stated ad counts for that allowance; only if none are stated do the fallback assumptions apply. Default: 20 paid leave days, plus zero additional paid holidays. This is a comparison proxy, not measured employee working time.

Annual cost = day rate × billed days. Paid leave keeps the day rate and bills all 261 weekdays. Month = annual cost ÷ 12; hour = day rate ÷ 8. Amounts are rounded for display. Monthly figures are budget averages, not retainers.
