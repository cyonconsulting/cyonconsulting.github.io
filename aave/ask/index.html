How the calculation works

221 planned days = 261 weekdays in 2027 − 5 public holidays − 35 leave days. Holidays and leave never overlap in this estimate.

Annual cost = day rate × billed days. Paid leave keeps the day rate and bills all 261 weekdays. Month = annual cost ÷ 12; hour = day rate ÷ 8. Amounts are rounded for display. Monthly figures are budget averages, not retainers.
