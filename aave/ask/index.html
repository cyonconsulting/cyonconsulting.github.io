How ten adverts become $306k

1 · Each advert as an hourly rate

  1. NBCUniversal $117/h
  2. Walmart $120/h
  3. MiniMed $143/h
  4. Consensys / MetaMask $149/h
  5. Zillow $163/h
  6. Reddit $178/h
  7. Slate Auto $185/h
  8. ServiceTitan $217/h
  9. Snap $227/h
  10. Google $234/h
  11. Mean $173.36/h

Hourly rate = top of the band ÷ (8 h × (261 weekdays − that employer’s paid days off). Where an employer states no count, the mean of those that do is used: 21 days of leave, 11.7 holidays.

2 · The hourly rate over my year

$173.36 × 8 h × 221 days

= $306,498 ≈ $306,000

Rounded to the nearest thousand. Preferred in SEK: 3,075,000 kr at the ECB fixing of 13 November 2026 ($1 = 10.05 kr); USD or EUR also possible.

3 · Why 221 days

261 − 35 − 5 = 221

Weekdays in 2027, minus seven weeks of leave and five Swedish public holidays that fall on weekdays. Those 40 days are unpaid: I invoice only the days I work.

What this is, and is not

The adverts are employee base salaries for Principal iOS and mobile roles at ten employers; each name above links to its source. They are market context: not a representative survey, not Aave’s budget, and not a contractor rate.
